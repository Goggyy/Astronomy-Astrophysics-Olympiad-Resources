# Astronomy-Astrophysics-Olympiad-Resources

An independent, student-led project to develop and share resources for astronomy and astrophysics olympiad preparation.

## Why I created this repository

While preparing for and participating in the IOAA, I found that there are limited resources and documentation for new students just starting out to learn more about the competitions cultures and the content of the olympiad, especially for students like me in New Zealand, where the IOAA is quite new. I am in the process of developing a series of resources that I would have found helpful when I was a student preparing for the IOAA, with the aim of making astronomy and astrophysics more accessible to other students.

## Projects

### Handouts

All the handouts I have worked on will be added to the [`handouts/`](handouts/) folder.

### IOAA Sky Map Trainer

I am developing an application to help students practise observational astronomy sky-map skills. The application has its **own repository**: [IOAA-Sky-Map](https://github.com/Goggyy/IOAA-Sky-Map).

### Historical IOAA results archive

I have collected historical IOAA results and plan to build a website with tables, graphs and statistics to display and compare this data. The website aims to give students an idea of their performance on past papers and a benchmark for assessing themelves in a constantly changing IOAA competition format.

This website will have a **separate repository**, it will be linked here when development finishes.
